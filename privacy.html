Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy covers Katha Town (our YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook Pages) and Katha Town Publisher, the internal tool we use to publish our own videos.

Katha Town Publisher (our publishing tool)

Katha Town Publisher is used only by the Katha Town team, to upload and manage videos on the Katha Town YouTube channels and Instagram accounts that we own. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it accesses: only our own Katha Town channels and accounts, with the permissions we grant when we connect them (uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, captions and playlists, and reading our own video statistics).
  • What it does not do: it does not access, collect or store any data about viewers or any other YouTube, Google, Instagram or Facebook users.
  • Storage: the access permissions for our own accounts are stored securely on our own server and are used only to publish our videos. We can revoke them at any time in our Google and Meta account settings.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Katha Town Publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers of our videos

We don't collect personal information from viewers. When you watch, like, follow or comment on our videos, that happens on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook and is governed by their own privacy policies. We may see the aggregate, anonymous statistics those platforms provide to all channel owners (for example, total views).

Our content is made for children and families. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
